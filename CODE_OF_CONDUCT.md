# Code of conduct

BOB contributors must keep project spaces professional, inclusive, and focused on constructive technical and scientific collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Give technical criticism with evidence, assume good faith while facts are incomplete, and respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or escalate serious concerns to the contributor's institution or GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical and without retaliation.
